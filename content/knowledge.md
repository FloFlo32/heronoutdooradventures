## About
Heron Outdoor Adventures is the premier Lake Allatoona fishing guide service near
Atlanta, Georgia, run by Pro Guide Joseph. With over 40 years of fishing experience,
Joseph and the team (Striper Mike and Catfish Jake) specialize in Striped Bass
(Stripers), Hybrids, Spotted Bass, Crappie, Bream, Catfish and all North Georgia
species on Lake Allatoona, Carters Lake, Lake Lanier, the Etowah River and the Coosa
River.

Joseph started guiding people on fishing adventures as early as age 15 on the Great
Lakes and Northeastern U.S. lakes and streams, and now guides out of Acworth, GA.
Heron Outdoor Adventures prides itself on instructional fishing, teaching the balance
of nature and conservation, relationship building, and creating memories that last a
lifetime. Trips are catch-and-release friendly.
